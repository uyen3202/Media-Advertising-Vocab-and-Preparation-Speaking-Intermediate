Vocabulary & Preparation

Before you start: Purpose, Task and Criteria for Success

Purpose: To help you not only recognise words but also remember and use Media & Advertising vocabulary when you speak, so you can move towards Band 6.5 in IELTS Speaking.

Task: (1) In class: Reading, Exercises 1-4 and Listening. (2) At home: Exercises 5-6 and Speaking Preparation (picture description). (3) Record your answers on this page, complete the Self-check on the last tab, then submit and send your link to the teacher. Reading, Exercises 1-4 and Listening are checked automatically when you submit; your speaking is marked by your teacher, who sends you a feedback link.

Learning objectives - by the end, I can:

  • Recognise and recall at least 9 key words/phrases of the topic (Exercises 1-4).
  • Use at least 6 topic words when speaking, including at least 3 of my own (not in the list).
  • Build an answer in 3 levels: a basic sentence, a sentence in my own words, a sentence with an example.
  • Describe a picture in the order Overview, Details, Opinion with at least 3 linking words.
  • Listen back to my recording and evaluate it with a checklist.

Criteria for success: Speak for the full time (30-60 seconds), use at least 6 topic words (3 of your own) and at least 4 different linking devices, and give a reason and an example. The Band 5.0 / 5.5 / 6.0 / 6.5 rubric is in the Speaking Test file.
